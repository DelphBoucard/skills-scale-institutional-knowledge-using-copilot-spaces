# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation of features against acceptance criteria. They work with developers and product managers to ensure features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Create and maintain test plans for features and releases
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage defects with severity and priority
- Collaborate on Definition of Done and quality gates
- Participate in pre-release smoke testing and go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Ensure quality standards are met before release
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable confidence in feature acceptance and delivery

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status updates in delivery syncs
- Defect reports and test coverage metrics
- Participation in sprint planning and release planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Work together to resolve defects and design testable solutions
- **Product Managers:** Validate that features meet acceptance criteria and user expectations
- **Project Managers:** Provide QA status and gate decisions for release readiness

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors represent business and stakeholder interests. They provide approvals, budget, and executive support for projects, and help remove organizational barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and major milestone gates
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Escalate risks and blockers that require executive intervention
- Communicate project status to broader leadership
- Allocate resources and resolve cross-organizational dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Enable teams to succeed by removing barriers

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Milestone gate reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Ad-hoc escalation for priority issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers:** Receive escalations and provide approval at key decision gates
- **Product Managers:** Collaborate on business value and strategic alignment
- **All Team Members:** Provide air cover and remove organizational obstacles

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical strategy, design guidance, and ensure solutions meet enterprise standards and scalability requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical design decisions
- Identify technical risks and mitigation strategies
- Ensure alignment with enterprise standards and best practices
- Guide integration with existing systems
- Mentor developers on architectural patterns and design

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk and rework
- Maintain consistency across projects

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk register updates for technical risks
- Guidance in sprint planning and backlog refinement

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Provide design guidance and review technical solutions
- **Project Managers:** Identify technical risks and mitigation timelines
- **QA/Testing Lead:** Ensure designs support comprehensive testing strategies

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical implementation by gathering requirements, documenting business logic, and ensuring feature design aligns with user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Create user stories and acceptance criteria
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather feedback
- Analyze process gaps and improvement opportunities
- Support UAT and user feedback validation

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real business and user needs
- Reduce rework due to misaligned requirements
- Enable clear communication between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user story refinement
- Stakeholder interviews and feedback sessions
- Participation in sprint planning and backlog grooming

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers:** Translate business needs into prioritized backlog items
- **Developers:** Clarify requirements and validate technical feasibility
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors:** Gather business context and validate requirements alignment
